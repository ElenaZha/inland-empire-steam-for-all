Program overview

Math, off the worksheet

Our Youth Math Club is open to any student in grades 6 through 8. Each week, members work in small teams on problems that don't show up on a typical homework sheet — logic puzzles, pattern-hunting, and the kind of multi-step questions that reward a good guess-and-check as much as a memorized formula.

The goal isn't just sharper test scores, though those tend to follow. It's helping kids see themselves as people who are good at math, out loud, in front of their friends. Members who want more of a challenge can try out for regional math competitions each year.
