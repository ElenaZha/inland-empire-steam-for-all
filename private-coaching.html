Private Coaching

One-on-one,
at your pace.

For students who want more focused attention than a group program allows, our private coaching pairs kids with a coach for personalized math or chess instruction — in person around the Inland Empire, or online.

Request a Coach Meet the Coaches

How it works

A coach matched to your kid

We start with a short intake call to understand where your child is and what they want more of — confidence with fractions, a shot at the school chess team, prep for a specific exam. From there, we match your family with one of our vetted coaches for weekly sessions, offered in 4- and 8-week blocks so you can try it out before committing further.

Rates & scheduling

Sessions are offered in 4- and 8-week blocks, priced $35–$55 per hour on a sliding scale. Need-based discounts are available — no family is turned away for inability to pay. 100% of revenue is reinvested into the program. In-person sessions are available across the Inland Empire; online sessions are available anywhere.
